Why This Summit Matters

The Industry Needs a New Playbook

The construction and infrastructure sector faces unprecedented challenges. This summit addresses the critical gaps that cost the industry billions.

35%

Project Cost Overruns

On average, large construction projects run 35% over budget and take 20% longer than planned. We address the root causes — leadership, planning, and execution gaps.

$12T

Global Construction Spending

The industry is projected to reach $12 trillion in annual spending. Yet productivity has barely improved in 20 years. We explore how leaders can capture this massive opportunity.

1.5%

Low R&D Adoption Rate

Construction spends just 1.5% of revenue on R&D — one of the lowest across all industries. The summit showcases how technology adoption can transform outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions from delegates, sponsors, and media about the Build Tek CORE Summit 2026.

What is Build Tek CORE Summit 2026?
Build Tek CORE Summit 2026 is an exclusive, invite-only leadership forum for senior decision-makers in Construction, Real Estate, and Infrastructure. It brings together 500+ C-suite executives, CTOs, developers, EPC contractors, and ConTech/PropTech leaders for two days of strategic dialogue, peer learning, and high-value networking — with zero sales pitches and no open registration.
When and where is the CORE Summit 2026?
The Build Tek CORE Summit 2026 takes place on 11–12 July 2026 in Pune, Maharashtra, India. Pune is strategically located between Mumbai and the wider Maharashtra construction ecosystem, making it the ideal base for India's most senior built-world leaders to converge.
Who should attend the CORE Summit?
The CORE Summit is designed for C-level and senior decision-makers in construction, real estate, and infrastructure — including CEOs, CTOs, CFOs, Directors, Developers & Promoters, EPC Contractors, Architects & Consultants, ConTech & PropTech innovators, Infrastructure Finance professionals, and Government/Policy leaders. Attendance is by invitation only; every delegate is hand-selected by the Build Tek team.
What topics are covered at the CORE Summit?
The CORE Summit covers six pillars: Leadership & Strategy, Project Excellence, Digital Transformation (BIM, IoT, AI, digital twins), Sustainability & ESG (green building, net-zero, BRSR), Future Technology (AI, robotics, 3D printing, drones), and Safety & Compliance. Sessions include keynote addresses, power panels, closed-door roundtables, and masterclasses — all designed for practitioners, not generalists.
How can my company sponsor the CORE Summit?
Sponsorship packages are available for organisations seeking direct access to 500+ senior construction, real estate, and infrastructure decision-makers. Tiers include Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Associate partnerships — each with defined brand visibility, speaking opportunities, and delegate access. Use the 'Become a Partner' button on this page or email partners@buildtek.in to request the full sponsorship deck.
How do I get an invitation to the CORE Summit?
Submit a request using the 'Request Invitation' form on this page. The Build Tek team reviews all applications and extends invitations to qualified senior decision-makers in the construction, real estate, and infrastructure sector. There is no open public registration — curation is what keeps the quality of peer exchange at the level our attendees expect.
